## Key Publications (First Author)

- **Seymour, R. A.,** Hardy, S., Pan, Y., & Dunkley, B. T. (2026). Quantifying Neural Stability: Validation of a New Brain Stability Index. [bioRxiv, 2026.08.21.746247.](https://doi.org/10.1101/2026.08.21.746247)

*Introduces and validates the Brain Stability Index, a new measure of the stability of neural activity.*

- **Seymour, R. A.,** Rippon, G., Gooding-Williams, G., Wang, H., & Kessler, K. (2025). The Neural Oscillatory Basis of Perspective-Taking in Autistic and Non-Autistic Adolescents Using Magnetoencephalography. [European Journal of Neuroscience, 61, e70109.](https://doi.org/10.1111/ejn.70109)

*Using MEG and an embodied perspective-taking task, we show that autistic adolescents show reduced theta power across social-cognitive brain regions and greater alpha decreases in visual cortex, suggesting they may rely on alternative strategies (e.g. object rotation) rather than embodied self-rotation.*

- **Seymour, R. A.,** Alexander, N., & Maguire, E. A. (2022). Robust estimation of 1/f activity improves oscillatory burst detection. [European Journal of Neuroscience, 56(10), 5836-5852.](https://doi.org/10.1111/ejn.15829)

*An improved method for detecting oscillatory bursts by estimating background 1/f activity more accurately. Code: [fBOSC](https://github.com/neurofractal/fBOSC).*

- **Seymour, R. A.,** Alexander, N., Mellor, S., O'Neill, G. C., Tierney, T. M., Barnes, G. R., & Maguire, E. A. (2022). Interference suppression techniques for OPM-based MEG: Opportunities and challenges. [NeuroImage, 247, 118834.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2021.118834)

*A tutorial-based review on issues with interference (noise) in wearable OPM-MEG recordings and how to fix these issues. We focus on software-based interference suppression and include two detailed tutorials ([code](https://github.com/FIL-OPMEG/tutorials_interference)).*

- Hepschke, J. L\*., **Seymour, R. A\***., He, W. A., Etchell, A., Sowman, P. F., & Fraser, C. (2022). Cortical oscillatory dysrhythmias in visual snow syndrome: A MEG study. [Brain Communications, 4(1), fcab296.](https://doi.org/10.1093/braincomms/fcab296) *\* Joint first authors.*

- **Seymour, R. A.,** Alexander, N., Mellor, S. J., O'Neill, G. C., Tierney, T. M., Barnes, G. R., & Maguire, E. A. (2021). Using OPMs to measure neural activity in standing, mobile participants. [NeuroImage, 244, 118604.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2021.118604)

*We show how wearable OPM-MEG can be used to measure neural activity while participants are standing and freely moving their head.*

- **Seymour, R. A.,** Rippon, G., Gooding-Williams, G., Sowman, P. F., & Kessler, K. (2020). Reduced auditory steady state responses in autism spectrum disorder. [Molecular Autism, 11(1), 1-13.](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13229-020-00357-y)

*Using MEG we show that autistic adolescents have reduced auditory steady-state responses at 40 Hz.*

- **Seymour, R. A.,** Rippon, G., Gooding-Williams, G., Schoffelen, J. M., & Kessler, K. (2019). Dysregulated oscillatory connectivity in the visual system in autism spectrum disorder. [Brain, 142(10), 3294-3305.](https://doi.org/10.1093/brain/awz214)

*Visual connectivity was examined in autistic adolescents using a simple grating stimulus. We found both reduced feedback connectivity and dysregulated local processing. Our results have implications for sensory sensitivities in autism and link to predictive-coding accounts of perception.*

- **Seymour, R. A.,** Wang, H., Rippon, G., & Kessler, K. (2018). Oscillatory networks of high-level mental alignment: A perspective-taking MEG study. [NeuroImage, 177, 98-107.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2018.05.016)

*Theta-band activity in the right temporo-parietal junction, coordinated with prefrontal and motor/body-schema regions, supports embodied perspective-taking.*

- **Seymour, R. A.,** Rippon, G., & Kessler, K. (2017). The Detection of Phase Amplitude Coupling during Sensory Processing. [Frontiers in Neuroscience, 11, 487.](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnins.2017.00487)

*Various methods for measuring Phase Amplitude Coupling (PAC) are reviewed and compared using simulated data and real MEG recordings. Practical recommendations for PAC analysis are given. Code: [PACmeg](https://github.com/neurofractal/pacmeg).*
